ElevenLabs audio

All music and sound effects in Scrappy Yard came from ElevenLabs text prompts, generated by Grok Bot on the box and wired into the game with a synth fallback if a file fails to load.

How it started

Chong-U (owner prompt) · Nov 4, 3:41 PM
let's install elevenlabs skills for music and sfx
Chong-U (owner prompt) · 3:45 PM
just use the eleven labs skills? i can then give u an api key
Chong-U (owner prompt) · 3:48 PM
can i give u securely

Yes: the key went in through Grok Bot's secure secret prompt and landed as an environment variable on the box. The bot never saw or printed the value. That's the pattern to copy for any API key: never paste it into chat.

The regen: from cartoon to industrial

Chong-U (owner prompt) · Nov 4, 7:43 PM
change sounds and when crashing i want a different sound than the clown sound

Grok Bot regenerated six files for the scrapyard menu: the four UI sounds, the crash and the game-over sting, plus a new menu theme. The prompts got explicit negatives: the crash now ends "realistic, no cartoon"; the game-over ends "ominous, no trombone, no cartoon". Jump, pickup and win stayed cartoony on purpose; the game is still family-friendly.

Sound as gameplay

  • sfx_stomp plays on a timer that gets louder and faster as the mech gap shrinks, so you hear the threat close in.
  • sfx_danger fires when a hazard is under 1.2 s away in your lane.
  • sfx_pickup steps up in pitch across a run of nuts.

Reuse in the trailer

The HyperFrames trailer is cut to bgm_play.mp3 at 150 BPM (one beat = 0.4 s), and the jump, pickup, hit and game-over SFX hit on the real in-game events.

Takeaways
  • Put tempo, mood, instruments and "loopable, no vocals" in music prompts.
  • Say what you don't want (no cartoon, no trombone). Negatives fixed the crash sound in one regen.
  • Keep a prompts file next to the audio so any sound can be regenerated or tweaked later.
  • Ship a fallback so a missing file never silences the game.
